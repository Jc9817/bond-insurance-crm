# Sending WhatsApp documents to DocFlow

When a customer sends a document on WhatsApp (an LOA, an SSM printout, a
photo of an IC), this is how to get it into DocFlow so it can be filed to the
right case. There is nothing to install.

## On your phone: upload straight to the DocFlow Inbox

1. In WhatsApp, open the chat and tap the document or photo.
2. Tap **⋮** (top right) → **Share** (Android), or the **share** icon (iPhone)
   → **Save to Files**. This saves it on your phone.
3. Open **DocFlow** in your phone's browser and log in.
4. Tap **Inbox** in the menu.
5. Tap the box that says **"Drop files or zips here, or click to choose"**,
   then pick the file you just saved. You can pick several at once.
6. The documents appear in the Inbox. From there, choose **Assign to Case**,
   or **Approve** if DocFlow already suggests where it goes.

If DocFlow says a file was **"already received before"**, someone has
already added that exact file. Nothing more to do.

## On the computer: WhatsApp Web or WhatsApp Desktop

1. Open the chat, hover over the document and click the **download** arrow.
2. Open DocFlow → **Inbox**.
3. Drag the downloaded file (or several, or a zip) onto the
   **"Drop files or zips here"** box.
