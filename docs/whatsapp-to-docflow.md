# Sending WhatsApp documents to DocFlow

When a customer sends a document on WhatsApp (an LOA, an SSM printout, a
photo of an IC), this is how to get it into DocFlow, onto the right case.
There is nothing to install.

## On your phone

1. In WhatsApp, open the chat and tap the document or photo.
2. Tap **⋮** (top right) → **Share** (Android), or the **share** icon (iPhone)
   → **Save to Files**. This saves it on your phone.
3. Open **DocFlow** in your phone's browser and log in.
4. Find the case: tap **Cases**, or type the customer or case number in the
   search box at the top of the menu.
5. In the case's document checklist, tap **+ Upload Document** and pick the
   file you just saved.
6. Choose which checklist document it is (e.g. Letter of Award) in the list
   next to the file.

If DocFlow says **"This exact file is already on this case"**, someone has
already added it. Tap **Cancel**; nothing more to do.

## On the computer: WhatsApp Web or WhatsApp Desktop

1. Open the chat, hover over the document and click the **download** arrow.
2. Open DocFlow, find the case, click **+ Upload Document** and choose the
   downloaded file.

## No case yet?

Create the case first (**Cases** → **+ New Case**), then upload the document
onto it.

## Or by email

You can also share from WhatsApp straight to
**operation@tridentmanagement.com.my**. The email appears on the **Emails**
page in DocFlow by itself, where its attachments are filed to the case.
